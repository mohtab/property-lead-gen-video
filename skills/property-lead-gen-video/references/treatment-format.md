# Treatment output and editorial checks

## Campaign setup

Summarize the property, buyer, primary action, language, placement, duration and supplied assets in a short paragraph. List only material assumptions and unresolved facts. Include claim-to-source notes once for shared facts; repeat only when a variation adds a new claim.

## Variation comparison

| Variation | Hook | Buyer motivation | Supporting proof | Visual emphasis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Life at the property | Exact proposed opening | Lived benefit | Approved feature | Human experience |
| What makes it distinctive | Exact proposed opening | Meaningful difference | Approved differentiator | Product and detail |
| The opportunity to act | Exact proposed opening | Practical fit | Verified offer or release | Product choice and next step |

Do not treat the labels above as approved on-screen copy.

## Per-variation deliverables

Provide a one-sentence premise, then two coordinated tables. Keep copy, visuals and editing instructions distinct.

### Message timeline

| Beat | Display in/out | Span | Exact on-screen copy | Fully readable in/out and hold | Shot IDs |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Distinction | 0.00–3.00 | 3.00 s | Property name + short promise | Explicit interval and duration | S01, etc. |

Include all eight beats with the canonical boundaries from SKILL.md. Identify persistent logos, identity labels and disclosures separately; count their visual and reading load. Put facts awaiting approval in square brackets and label the output provisional. Never make an unresolved line look release-ready.

### Shot timeline

| Shot | Edit in/out | Duration | Picture or action | Asset filename and source in/out | Status |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| S01 | Exact interval | Seconds | Concrete composition | Inspected source or suggested capture | Available / unverified / missing |

Include a row for every shot and the closing card. Use source in/out timecodes only for inspected clips. When exact source trims are unavailable, write “trim to confirm”; distinguish these from the planned edit timecodes. Confirm contiguous coverage from 0 to 25 seconds. Map a shot crossing a message boundary to both beats.

List missing assets after the timeline with practical alternatives, such as another inspected interior angle. If only still renders exist, propose restrained crops and pans within the actual images. Do not invent camera travel or unseen rooms. If only a brochure exists, specify shot requirements and label the asset plan provisional.

When a limited asset set cannot sustain 12–15 meaningful shots, use fewer longer views and editorial cards and explain the change. Do not substitute invented imagery to hit a shot count. If no human footage is available, retain the Lifestyle purpose through experiential copy over a relevant real asset; label the human scene as a suggested capture, not an available shot. A typography card can communicate an approved feature but does not count as visual proof of that feature.

### Editor notes

Specify typography hierarchy, text position, transitions, movement, audio direction and closing card. Use supplied brand rules. Suggest simple text fades of about 0.15–0.25 seconds where appropriate. Keep text steady through image cuts. Recommend music direction without claiming to supply or license a track. Keep all core information understandable on mute; add optional voiceover only when useful or requested.

## Reading time

Treat these as starting heuristics and verify the actual copy:

| Text burden | Suggested fully readable hold |
|---|---:|
| 3–5 simple words | 2–2.5 s |
| 6–8 words | 3–3.5 s |
| 9–12 words | 4–5 s |

Account for entrance/exit animation, unfamiliar names, qualifiers and numbers. Within the standard 2-second availability slot, use a very short 2–3 word message and little or no text animation. Within the 2.5-second place slot, keep location wording brief. A 3.5-second slot with 0.2-second entrance and exit permits 3.1 seconds of full readability. Do not report 3.5 seconds as the reading hold in that case.

Prefer showing the final price immediately. Retain the currency and relevant basis, for example total sale price versus monthly rent. Do not let disclaimers become unreadable to preserve a headline. If required wording cannot fit, propose a longer version and explain the specific constraint.

## Input shortcuts

Accept “brochure + asset folder + priority buyer + desired action” as the minimum practical start. Extract the rest before asking. If no brand system is supplied, label a neutral typographic treatment as a proposal. If no CTA is supplied, propose an action consistent with the objective, and mark its route for confirmation.

Use public research only when requested or necessary to resolve an external fact. Cite researched facts and keep them distinct from campaign approval. Do not treat research as authorization to publish a price or claim.

## Adaptation examples

- Residential sales: residence types, approved sale price or release, availability, appointment.
- Residential leasing: unit options, approved monthly or annual rent, verified availability, viewing.
- Office leasing: space types, approved terms, availability, site tour.
- Off-plan launch: approved design features and renders, release status, registration. Do not imply completed amenities or immediate occupation.

When there is no factual urgency, use a confirmed service or current release status rather than “last chance” or “limited stock.” If no such fact is supported, retain an approval placeholder; the eight-beat format is not permission to fabricate.
